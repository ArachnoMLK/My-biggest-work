# Legal notes

*Not legal advice. Written by the project, to keep everyone on the safe side.*

- **Unofficial.** Brawlhalla is developed by Blue Mammoth Games and published by Ubisoft.
  This project is not affiliated with or endorsed by them. Names and trademarks belong to their
  owners.
- **No game content is distributed.** The repository contains only original code and research
  notes. Tools read the user's own, legally obtained installation, and write their outputs to a
  local, git-ignored `work/` folder.
- **Interoperability purpose.** The aim is to view one's own replay files outside the game.
  The game's terms of use have not been reviewed by the project yet; contributors should read them
  ([Ubisoft Terms of Use](https://legal.ubi.com/termsofuse/)) and decide for themselves.
  In the EU, decompilation for interoperability is allowed only under strict conditions
  (Directive 2009/24/EC, art. 6).
- **No cheating.** Nothing here touches the running game, online services or anti-cheat systems,
  and contributions that do will be refused.
- **Takedown.** If the rights holders object to any content, open an issue or contact the owner:
  it will be reviewed and removed promptly.
- **Privacy.** Replay files contain player names. Do not publish replays or per-player data;
  share only aggregated statistics.
